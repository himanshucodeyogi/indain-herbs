Current site me already **multi-species products, research-backed positioning, certifications, careers, blog/news, and contact flows** visible hain — lekin unhe nayi site me much stronger UX aur conversion system me organize karna chahiye. # 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Indian Herbs ko ek **modern, high-trust, research-led, export-ready, multi-species phytogenic animal healthcare brand** ke roop me present karne ke liye ek nayi website build karni hai.

Current website me brand ki strength visible hai:

* 1951 legacy
* herbal animal healthcare positioning
* research & innovation claims
* certifications
* species-based products
* careers and contact pages
* product commerce/catalog structure
  Lekin user journey, brand clarity, product discovery, trust hierarchy, export conversion, and B2B lead capture ko aur strong banana zaroori hai. ([Indian Herbs][1])

### Final Goal:

Ek aisi website build karni jo:

1. **Brand authority build kare**
2. **Scientific trust establish kare**
3. **Products ko easily discoverable banaye**
4. **Domestic + export leads generate kare**
5. **Distributors, vets, institutions aur end-buyers ke liye relevant ho**
6. **SEO-friendly aur scalable ho**

# 14. CONTENT REQUIREMENTS

## Content Tone

* Scientific but understandable
* Global B2B ready
* Evidence-backed
* Trustworthy
* Not overly “spiritual Ayurveda”
* More “nature-inspired science”

## Writing Guidelines

Avoid overclaim-heavy copy like:

* “No.1”
* “best in world”
* “world class”
  unless verified and strategically approved.

Use stronger, cleaner language like:

* Research-backed phytogenic solutions
* Evidence-led herbal animal health
* Sustainable productivity support
* Nature-inspired, science-validated formulations

# 17. TECH RECOMMENDATION

## Recommended Build Options

### Best for scale:

* **Next.js / React + Headless CMS**
  or
* **WordPress (custom theme)**
  or
* **Webflow + CMS** (if content-heavy, lower engineering complexity)

## My Recommendation

If long-term scale, SEO, product catalog and performance important hain:

> **Next.js + Sanity / Strapi / WordPress Headless**

If fast marketing website chahiye:

> **Webflow / Framer + CMS**

If team traditional admin chahti hai:

> **Custom WordPress**
